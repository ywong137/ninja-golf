# Combat recordings

These recordings use CC0 1.0 or CC BY-SA 3.0, as listed below. No music was generated.

- Sword swishes and clashes: StarNinjas, [Sword sounds](https://opengameart.org/comment/92361). Files sword.1–3.ogg and sword_clash.1–3.ogg, renamed without audio edits.
- Archived punches and wood-crunch layers: Kenney, [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds). Files impactPunch_heavy_000–002.ogg and impactWood_heavy_000–001.ogg, renamed without audio edits.

- Flesh impacts: Iwan ‘qubodup’ Gabovitch, [Impact](https://opengameart.org/content/impact), CC0. qubodupImpactMeat01.ogg and qubodupImpactMeat02.ogg, renamed flesh-1.ogg and flesh-2.ogg without audio edits.

The engine layers, varies playback pitch, and limits concurrent voices.
