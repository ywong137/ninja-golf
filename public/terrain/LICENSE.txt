REGIONAL ELEVATION DATA

The Japanese and Scottish distant landscapes use adapted Mapzen Terrain Tiles.
The Arizona landscape uses a finer USGS National Map 3DEP elevation crop. The game changes placement, horizontal scale, elevation and the course
surroundings. The fictional golf courses are not surveys of real golf courses.

REGIONAL COLOR IMAGERY

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2024.
The Scottish and Arizona distant surfaces use cropped Sentinel-2 L2A true-color
images. The game reprojects and resizes the source images, then blends their
colors with scanned ground detail and fictional terrain near each course.
ESA produces the Sentinel data. Sinergise and Element 84 process the public
cloud-optimized imagery hosted by AWS. IMAGERY.json records scene identifiers,
source URLs, observation dates, crop bounds, output sizes and checksums.
