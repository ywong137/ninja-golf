REGIONAL ELEVATION DATA

The Japanese, Scottish and Arizona distant landscapes use adapted Mapzen Terrain
Tiles. The game changes placement, horizontal scale, elevation and the course
surroundings. The fictional golf courses are not surveys of real golf courses.
