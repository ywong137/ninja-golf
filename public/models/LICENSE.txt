The six heroes and four source enemy roles derive from the licensed bodies,
faces, clothing meshes, and textures. Three enemy wardrobes reuse these bodies.
The cloth ninja adds original weighted wraps and generated cloth artwork.
The runtime assigns four fixed palettes to each wardrobe, independently of role.
Native motion and hand markers fit each original skeleton.
Source identities and build details: docs/rocketbox-roster.md.
Enemy wardrobe sources and artwork: assets/enemies/SOURCES.md.

The Vice President adapts the native head geometry and texture to Ethan Cary's
authorized likeness, with original glasses. The Closer uses an edited forehead
texture and fringe geometry. These adaptations retain the licensed source rigs.

The Ace rising attack adapts Sword_Regular_A and Sword_Regular_A_Rec from
Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2, CC0. Native joints, foot contacts,
timing, transitions, and root travel were re-authored for this game.

The Ace golf finish uses measured pose references from Carnegie Mellon
University's Motion Capture Database, subject 64, trial 64_01, frame 409.
https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
The database permits use in products, but prohibits direct resale of its data.
The reference pose was mirrored and refitted to the native skeleton and club.
The resulting animation is an adaptation, not a direct motion capture replay.

All six hero backswings also use pose references from CMU subject 64, trial
64_01, frames 180 through 330. The source motion was mirrored, scaled, and
refitted to each native skeleton with a shared two-hand club grip.

All six heroes' forward running and sprinting adapt CMU subject 09, trial
09_01, frames 2 through 90 at 120 Hz. The shared conversion fits each body's
proportions, retains native hand motion, and adds shoe contacts and joint limits.
Source skeleton: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/09/09.asf
Source recording: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/09/09_01.amc
Usage terms: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/faqs.php

The Ace opening cut adapts the complete Sword_Attack clip from Quaternius
Universal Animation Library 1, CC0. It retains the source preparation, step,
body turn, balancing arm, and recovery. Native joint frames and the sword
grip fit the Rocketbox skeleton. Gameplay scales the complete source clock.

Ronin_Power_Cut and Ethan_Naginata_Power_Cut adapt Adobe Mixamo's
Great Sword Power Slash, downloaded through the owner's Adobe account.
The retarget preserves the full body performance and fits the two hands,
weapon mount, native elbow/knee frames, and foot contacts to these heroes.
Only the adapted game animations ship. The source FBX files remain local.
Adobe Mixamo FAQ: https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html

2026-10-03: Ronin_Driving_Cut and Ethan_Naginata_Driving_Cut derive from Adobe Mixamo Great Sword Downward Slash. Source files remain private; game assets contain fitted motion on the existing character rigs.

The Hustler opening cut adapts Sword_Regular_B and Sword_Regular_B_Rec from
Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2, CC0. The full-body attack retains
its matching authored recovery. Native joint frames, the closed sword hand,
and target-directed head motion fit the existing character mesh.

The Closer opening light cut (Closer_Rising_Cut) adapts Sword_Regular_A and
Sword_Regular_A_Rec from Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2 (CC0).
The full-body movement retains the source step, turn, and authored recovery.
The native rig uses bounded forearm rotation and a neutral sword wrist.

The Closer three-cut light combo adapts the continuous Sword_Regular_Combo
from Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2 (CC0). The opening and return
cuts use the matching A_Rec and B_Rec recoveries when no input is queued.
Source steps and torso turns remain connected at shared source frames.
Horizontal pelvis travel moves the gameplay actor; native bone scales stay
constant across the extracted clips. The original character mesh is unchanged.

The Closer heavy finish and four-cut musou adapt Sword_Heavy_Combo from
Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2 (CC0). The native transfer retains
the source torso turns, steps, and recovery. A continuous elbow plane avoids
the near-straight arm flip; a short landing phase plays more slowly. The
weapon remains attached to the closed hand throughout the performance.

The Ronin advancing musou adapts Adobe Mixamo Great Sword Combo Slash.
The complete three-cut performance retains its body motion and recovery.
The native transfer fits a 17 cm two-handed grip and extracts horizontal travel.
The fixed blade mount, timing, and game integration are Ninja Golf modifications.

2026-10-03: Ace_Turning_Double_Cut derives from Adobe Mixamo Great Sword Power Slash. The game uses fitted motion on the existing licensed character; the standalone source file remains private.

were generated for Ninja Golf from the approved outfit concepts.

through each character's original bind frames. Major Threat also adds knee and
