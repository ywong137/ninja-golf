# Contact effects and Closer cloth

The Closer's front hem now uses 28 mm of clearance and the 99th percentile thigh radius. Other costumes retain their previous values. The existing one-sided constraint keeps the hem on its original side of the thigh. A two-thigh trial folded the cloth and was rejected.

The musou overlay leaves the central face area clear. Gold edge rays replace the black rays. The diagonal slashes also leave a clear center. All six native facial targets remain active. The Closer additionally uses her angry texture directly on the native face, delivered by the preceding release.

Contact flashes now use one bounded draw with 96 slots. Bursts stay at the struck body. They include a hot center, broad orange rays, and an expanding broken ring. Spark streaks are longer and wider. Short bloom appears only while contact flashes remain active. Returning to golf clears these effects.

The previous audio mix played a wood impact before the cutting layers. Sword and clash recordings contained about 130–150 ms of lead-in. Contact playback now skips that lead-in, reduces wood gain, and combines two flesh layers with the cutting transient. Short gain fades prevent clicks at trimmed endpoints. The recordings retain their existing CC0 credits.

Reference: [SW5 musou collection](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IkA8oEUrSX8). Inspection used muted playback and saved reference frames. The design follows its broad contact bursts and long spark trails.

Validation:

- Ten focused tests pass, including bounded flash storage, contact position, cleanup, cloth clearance, and face material ownership.
- Browser combat check decodes ten recordings, completes an attack-to-run transition, and reports no errors.
- All six native musou faces reach full expression during the real cinematic clock.
- The Closer's stance and eight light-attack samples were reviewed visually.
- A 64-enemy heavy-attack test averaged 49.2 FPS at 1440 × 900, render ratio 1, on an Apple M1 Max. Its 95th-percentile frame time was 33.4 ms. This is one local scene.
- All browser audio was muted. Audio mixes were rendered offline.

The game still has visible art and animation limitations. This update does not establish AAA quality.
