# Layout identity audit

Reviewed all 36 authored plans and their shared curved coverage after deployment `0060b61`. Distinct names and different seeds do not establish distinct layouts.

The collection has useful variety, but several holes repeat the same topology and shot decision. The desert opener previously repeated the Highland opener's divided fairway loop. The first change replaced that desert opener. The next change replaces Desert 2 and Desert 6.

## Desert opener correction

Saguaro Sunrise now has three separate turf areas: a short tee runway, an oblique landing shelf, and a detached final approach. It has no fairway fork or enclosing loop.

The wide left target needs a 154 m carry and provides a clear 20 m landing radius. It leaves approximately 188 m to the cup. The narrow right target needs a 201 m carry over a waste bunker. Its clear landing radius is 9 m, but it leaves only 142 m. The second waste bunker makes the direct central line less forgiving.

Dry rough separates the turf areas. The walking route reaches every landing shelf without water or bridges. The centerline's maximum sampled grade is 10.6%. The course remains par 4, with a 414 m authored route and 328 m direct tee-to-cup distance. Tee and cup coordinates do not change.

The shared smoothing, terrain rules, map outlines, and physics remain unchanged. Tests count actual turf components and check clear landing discs, carry hazards, approach distances, route grades, and cup clearance.

## Proposed yardage correction — not implemented

`applyAuthoredLayouts` assigns `playLength` from `layout.route`. That route serves walking and scenery placement. The course card and hole HUD then convert that length to yards. This makes a bank detour or spiral look like required shot distance.

Keep `layout.route` unchanged. Add an explicit `layout.shotLine`, with tee, intended landing points, and cup in world metres. For par-3 holes, use the direct tee-to-cup segment. For longer holes, author the normal shot route through strategic landing areas. Do not automatically follow every fairway bend or select the shortest possible shortcut.

Calculate `walkingLength` from `layout.route` and `playingLength` from `layout.shotLine`. Change the two UI yardage consumers to `playingLength`. Keep distance-to-pin as the existing direct distance. Remove the ambiguous `playLength` field after its consumers migrate.

For the revised short holes, displayed playing distances would become approximately 174 yd and 151 yd. Their walking distances would remain 209 yd and 231 yd. Cyber 6 would use its direct par-3 carry instead of displaying its 508 m spiral walk. Cyber 7 needs an authored two-landing shot line instead of its 1,091 m walking route.

Validate shot-line endpoints, dry intended landing areas, plausible club reach, and course totals. Add a UI assertion that par-3 yardage equals direct tee-to-cup distance. Review all longer-hole shot lines before migration; a blanket direct-distance replacement would conceal real dogleg length.
